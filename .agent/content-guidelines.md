# Content Guidelines — The Tooth Dental Studio

**Writing and content rules for AI agents creating website copy.**

This document defines how to write for The Tooth Dental Studio website. All content facts must reference `.agent/CANONICAL.md`.

---

## Voice

### The voice should feel:

| Attribute         | Definition                                                      |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Calm**          | Measured, unhurried, reassuring presence                        |
| **Confident**     | Assured without arrogance, credible without boasting            |
| **Human**         | Warm, approachable, personal — never corporate or clinical-cold |
| **Professional**  | Competent, reliable, trustworthy                                |
| **Reassuring**    | Addresses patient anxiety, reduces uncertainty                  |
| **Clear**         | Plain language, no jargon without explanation                   |
| **Patient-first** | Every sentence serves the patient's understanding and comfort   |

### The voice must never feel:

- Exaggerated or hyperbolic
- Salesy or pushy
- Corporate or institutional
- Cold or clinical
- Anxious or desperate
- Generic dental marketing

### Tone variations:

The voice remains consistent, but tone adapts to context:

| Context                | Tone                                   |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Hero                   | Confident, welcoming, reassuring       |
| Emergency              | Urgent but calm, responsive, available |
| Treatment explanations | Clear, informative, careful            |
| Patient stories        | Warm, authentic, human                 |
| Doctor section         | Professional, personal, credible       |
| Conversion             | Clear, direct, inviting — never pushy  |

## Patient Experience

### Narrative principle:

**Patient experience is a recurring theme, not a single testimonial section.**

The patient-experience narrative should weave through the entire website, reinforced through:

- Section copy
- Visual storytelling
- Review excerpts
- Structural elements (Listen → Explain → Treat → Follow Up)
- Tone and voice

### Repeated themes:

These themes should appear throughout the website in different forms:

| Theme                  | How to express                                                            |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Calm care**          | "calm and reassuring manner," "composure," "gentle"                       |
| **Clear explanations** | "clearly explained," "understanding the problem," "knowing your options"  |
| **Comfort**            | "comfortable," "feel at ease," "reassured"                                |
| **Prompt response**    | "attended to me at 12 AM," "prompt attention," "when they needed it most" |
| **Personal attention** | "personal attention," "someone is paying attention," "individual needs"   |
| **Follow-up**          | "stay connected after treatment," "supported beyond the appointment"      |

### Theme distribution:

Do not concentrate all patient-experience content in one section. Instead:

- Hero: Establishes the promise
- Philosophy: Defines the approach
- Authority: Proves the experience through reviews
- Doctor: Humanizes the care
- Care Method: Structures the experience
- Patient Stories: Brings the experience to life
- Throughout: Reinforced through copy and design

## Review Usage

### Rules for using patient reviews:

**Do:**

- Use real patient review language accurately
- Quote review excerpts exactly as written (with grammatical tolerance)
- Attribute reviews to named patients when available
- Use review count and rating as social proof
- Reference the themes patients consistently mention

**Do not:**

- Fabricate testimonials
- Paraphrase in a way that changes meaning
- Create composite or invented patient stories
- Make universal medical guarantees based on individual reviews
- Claim the review count proves objective clinical superiority

### Review framing:

| Acceptable                                                                                  | Not acceptable                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Patients have shared experiences of receiving prompt attention when they needed it most."  | "We guarantee 24/7 emergency care with immediate response."                |
| "The Tooth Dental Studio has earned a 5.0-star Google rating across 1,147 reviews."         | "The Tooth Dental Studio is the highest-rated dental clinic in Hyderabad." |
| "Patient reviews repeatedly describe Dr. Imran as calm, gentle, approachable and thorough." | "Dr. Imran is the most gentle dentist you will ever meet."                 |

### Verified review excerpts:

Use only approved review excerpts from the content foundation:

| Reviewer              | Excerpt                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Spoorthi K.           | "I was very nervous initially, but his composure and professionalism made the entire experience feel smooth and comfortable." |
| Sridevi Reddy Aellala | "He was kind and patient with kids."                                                                                          |
| Hamza Mohammed        | "He attended to me at 12 AM... and made sure I was comfortable throughout the procedure."                                     |

Additional review excerpts must be verified before use.

## Medical Copy

### Never promise:

| Do not claim                  | Why                                                                |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Guaranteed painless treatment | Cannot be guaranteed; varies by patient and procedure              |
| Guaranteed outcomes           | Medical outcomes depend on individual factors                      |
| 100% success                  | No medical procedure has 100% success rate                         |
| Instant results               | Treatment times vary; sets unrealistic expectations                |
| "Best" claims                 | Unless explicitly supported and approved; often legally restricted |

### Prefer careful language:

| Instead of                  | Use                                                                   |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Painless treatment"        | "Comfortable treatment experience" / "Focused on patient comfort"     |
| "Guaranteed results"        | "Treatment tailored to your individual needs"                         |
| "Best dentist in Hyderabad" | "Patient-focused dental care in Tolichowki, Hyderabad"                |
| "Instant relief"            | "Prompt assessment and care"                                          |
| "Permanent solution"        | "Designed to last" / "Long-term results depend on individual factors" |

### Approved medical language patterns:

| Context              | Approved phrasing                                                                  |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Treatment necessity  | "may be recommended when..."                                                       |
| Treatment planning   | "depends on the patient's oral health, bone condition and individual requirements" |
| Outcomes             | "treatment plan tailored to the condition"                                         |
| Assessment           | "A dental assessment helps determine whether..."                                   |
| Individual variation | "Treatment plans are based on individual dental needs"                             |

### Emergency care language:

The emergency section should convey availability and responsiveness without making absolute guarantees:

| Acceptable                                                                                                                         | Not acceptable                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| "Patients have shared experiences of reaching Dr. Imran during the night and receiving prompt attention when they needed it most." | "We are available 24/7 for all emergencies." |
| "Prompt dental assessment can help determine the right next step."                                                                 | "Immediate emergency care guaranteed."       |

**Note:** The verification note from CANONICAL.md must be preserved: "Current operating policy must be verified with the clinic before production launch."

## Copy Hierarchy

### Structural elements:

| Element                   | Purpose                            | Treatment                                          |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Eyebrow**               | Section label, context, category   | Small uppercase, concise, often 1-5 words          |
| **H1 / Main headline**    | Primary statement, emotional hook  | Large, commanding, signature phrase or key message |
| **H2 / Section headline** | Section focus, narrative anchor    | Substantial, clear, guides the section             |
| **Supporting headline**   | Secondary statement, amplification | Smaller than H1, complements main headline         |
| **Body copy**             | Explanation, detail, narrative     | Readable, warm, clear paragraphs                   |
| **Microcopy**             | Small labels, captions, metadata   | Brief, functional, often uppercase                 |
| **CTA**                   | Action prompt                      | Clear, direct, premium feel                        |

### Hierarchy principles:

**Eyebrow:**

- Establishes context before the headline
- Often indicates section type or category
- Examples: "THE PATIENT EXPERIENCE", "1,147 PATIENT VOICES", "DENTAL TREATMENTS IN TOLICHOWKI"

**H1 / Main headline:**

- The signature statement of the page or section
- Should feel like a declaration, not a description
- Examples: "DENTISTRY WITHOUT THE FEAR.", "THE EXPERIENCE IS PART OF THE TREATMENT."

**H2 / Section headline:**

- Anchors each section
- Should be memorable and meaningful
- Examples: "The treatment is only part of the experience.", "A reputation built one experience at a time."

**Supporting headline:**

- Amplifies or qualifies the main headline
- Creates layered messaging
- Example: "Calm care. Clear explanations. A better dental experience."

**Body copy:**

- Explains, narrates, provides detail
- Should be scannable but rewarding to read fully
- Use short paragraphs for scannability
- Maintain warm, clear voice throughout

**Microcopy:**

- Labels, captions, metadata
- Functional but not careless
- Examples: "5.0 ★★★★★", "Patient-rated dental care in Tolichowki, Hyderabad"

**CTA:**

- Action-oriented but not aggressive
- Should feel like an invitation, not a demand
- Examples: "Book an Appointment", "Talk to the Dentist →", "Call for Urgent Care"

## Content Reuse

### Rule:

**Do not repeat identical paragraphs throughout the page.**

Each section should have distinct copy that serves its specific purpose.

### Repetition vs. reinforcement:

| Do not do                                                    | Do instead                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Copy-paste the same paragraph in multiple sections           | Express the same concept differently for each context         |
| Repeat "5.0-star Google rating" identically in every section | Reference the rating in ways that serve the section's purpose |
| Use the same sentence structure repeatedly                   | Vary sentence structure while maintaining voice               |

### Concept reinforcement:

It is acceptable—and often necessary—to reinforce key concepts throughout the website. The key is to express them differently:

| Concept            | Section 01 expression                                        | Section 03 expression                                                                       | Section 14 expression                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Patient experience | "Calm care. Clear explanations. A better dental experience." | "Patient reviews repeatedly describe Dr. Imran as calm, gentle, approachable and thorough." | "For The Tooth Dental Studio, every appointment is an opportunity to make dental care feel more comfortable, more understandable and more personal." |

| Concept             | Section 01 expression    | Section 03 expression                                                               |
| ------------------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 5.0 / 1,147 reviews | Hero trust proof element | "The Tooth Dental Studio has earned a 5.0-star Google rating across 1,147 reviews." |

## Final Rule

**AI should improve presentation and readability, but must not overwrite the approved business strategy with generic AI-generated dental marketing copy.**

### What AI can do:

- Improve sentence flow and readability
- Ensure consistent voice throughout
- Enhance scannability and structure
- Fix grammar and clarity issues
- Optimize formatting for the web

### What AI must not do:

- Rewrite approved copy into generic dental marketing language
- Add marketing claims not in the approved content
- Replace patient-experience positioning with clinical-only messaging
- Invent statistics, reviews, or medical claims
- Change the brand voice to something more corporate or salesy
- Remove the human, warm, reassuring qualities of the approved copy

### Test for appropriateness:

After generating or editing copy, ask:

1. Does this preserve the approved content from CANONICAL.md?
2. Does this maintain the patient-experience positioning?
3. Does this avoid invented claims or generic marketing language?
4. Is this voice calm, confident, human, and reassuring?
5. Does this serve the patient's understanding and comfort?

If any answer is no, revise the copy.
